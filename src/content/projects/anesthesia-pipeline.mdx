## Thesis

General anesthesia is fundamentally a **multi-target binding problem**. Halothane simultaneously potentiates K2P channels, GABA-A receptors, and GluCl channels; antagonizes nAChR; blocks Complex I; and reduces SNARE-mediated synaptic release — at the same clinical concentration. No single-target model captures the phenotype. **Wave P** is a digital pharmacology pipeline that takes the multi-target framing seriously: dock 6 clinical anesthetics + 8 negative controls against 30 *C. elegans* Tier-1 anesthetic targets, propagate occupancies through mechanism-class-specific kinetic shifts, and feed the result into a network perturbation manager — **with honest scope labels per phase and documented boundaries where the binding-pipeline alone is insufficient.**

## Pipeline architecture

A → B → C → D → E/F → G is the canonical chain:

- **Phase A (shipped)** — UniProt → AlphaFold DB v6 latestVersion API → fpocket pocket detection. **30/32 Tier-1 targets** retrieved (NCA-1, UNC-80 missing from AF DB; ColabFold T4 fallback deferred per R14 mitigation on 8 GB VRAM).
- **Phase B (shipped)** — AutoDock Vina 1.1.2 docking via Meeko receptor + ligand prep. **540 dockings** (6 ligands × 30 targets × 3 poses).
- **Phase C (shipped)** — Vina ΔG → predicted Kd → Hill-equation occupancy with K_p amplification (halothane K_p = 250, etc.).
- **Phase D (shipped)** — kinetic-shift translation per mechanism class → `wave2_overlay.json` (v1) and `wave2_overlay_v2.json` (post-allosteric correction).
- **Phase E / F (scaffolded)** — Markov synapse SSA + metabolic ATP layer. Phase F's gas-1 hypersensitivity ratio is structurally **parameter-locked** (CP1) — block_factor cancels in d_WT / d_g1 ratio analytically; the 2.48× value lands in Morgan & Sedensky's 2-3× band by tuning, not by anesthetic-specific signal.
- **Phase G (in-progress)** — Brian2 LIF network perturbation manager + halothane dose-response demo on a 50-neuron LIF substrate. LIFBrain integration with the full 300-neuron *C. elegans* connectome is the next bet; behavioral threshold calibration is the documented gap.

## Calibration — the CP1-CP8 rigor pass

The original v1 pipeline reported a **5/5 PASS** verdict against published anesthesia phenotypes. A subsequent eight-checkpoint rigor pass downgraded that framing and produced a more honest **7 verified · 1 homolog-grounded · 5 awaiting wet-lab · 3 uncalibrated · 2 boundary FAIL** verdict structure. Highlights:

- **CP1** — Phase F's predicted gas-1 hypersensitivity ratio is invariant to the anesthetic-specific input across 19× variation in `block_factor`. Analytical proof: the (1−bf) term cancels in the d_WT / d_g1 ratio. Verdict downgraded `PASS_5/6 → PASS_PARAMETER_TUNED`.
- **CP4** — all 30 ground-truth anchors are functional EC50/IC50 from electrophysiology + mitochondrial assays. **Zero strict-Kd entries.** Directness-tier framework (T1 / T2 / T3) introduced; T1 strict subset (n=17) signed median log_err = +0.399 — consistent with PAM allosteric coupling η ≈ 0.4 (Forman & Miller 2016 PMID 27749338).
- **CP5** — single-parameter allosteric correction `f_allo = 10^0.399 = 2.50×` shifts T1 within-10× from **76% → 94%**. Leave-one-anesthetic-out cross-validation (mean held-out signed = +0.097) confirms the correction generalizes.
- **CP6** — twk-18 anchor direction was **inverted** in the original preregistration (claimed RESISTANT; real biology per Singaram 2011 PMID 22137475 says K2P GoF is HYPERSENSITIVE). Corrected.
- **CP7** — per-chemical-class stratification post-correction: 4 of 5 classes 100% within 10×. IV_PHENOL (propofol) retains a 67% within-10× rate driven by propofol's unusually strong GABA-A allosteric coupling (η_allo ≈ 0.02 vs typical 0.1-0.3).

## Boundary findings (Eger non-immobilizer puzzle)

Two explicit boundary tests document where the binding pipeline operates honestly as a *lipophilic-pocket-fit detector* rather than an *anesthetic-specificity detector*:

- **CP3** — cis-1,2-dichloroethylene (anesthetic per Eger 2001) vs trans-1,2-dichloroethylene (non-anesthetic). Maximum engagement gap across 0.1-30 mM aqueous concentrations: **0**. Pipeline cannot distinguish stereoisomers of small halogenated alkanes.
- **CP7** — hexafluoroethane (Eger non-immobilizer) engages **30/30** *C. elegans* targets at 1 mM, vs cis-DCE's **22/30**. The Eger non-immobilizer has the *stronger* binding profile.

These are **boundary findings, not pipeline failures**. The Eger non-immobilizer puzzle has been a known mystery in anesthesia research for ~30 years — single-pose docking on small lipophilic molecules cannot solve it. Anesthetic specificity must emerge at the network and behavioral threshold layers; Phase G is the next bet for that test.

## Methodology rigor

The Wave P methodology pattern is **systematic pre-flight pushback** before launching computational work blocks: enumerate every parameter, audit every citation against primary sources, ask whether the prediction is sensitive to its inputs, identify boundary tests *before* execution. Across this project's history this pattern has caught:

- 6 wrong / fabricated PMIDs in the original target CSV (Crowder 1996, Morgan & Sedensky, Sedensky 1992, van Swinderen 1999 mis-cited, Sedensky 2001 fabricated, Boddington 2017 fabricated)
- 30 / 32 wrong UniProt IDs
- 1 structural parameter-lock (Phase F)
- 1 inverted biological direction (twk-18 GoF)
- 1 calibration ground-truth conflation (Kd vs EC50)
- 1 mid-flight saturation collapse (Phase E)

Five case studies documenting these patterns are drafted (~6,700 words total) — links above in the Methodology panel. The umbrella thesis: **structured pre-flight pushback is cost-effective methodology for AI-assisted scientific work, particularly for work that grounds claims in primary sources, depends on parameter calibration, or makes direction-sensitive biological predictions.**
