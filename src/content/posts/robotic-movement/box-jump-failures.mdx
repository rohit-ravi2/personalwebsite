Part of the [Robotic Movement](/posts/robotic-movement/) series. The undisturbed landing, and how the jump was built, is in [Designing a robot jump](/posts/robotic-movement/jumping/).

## What these clips are

This is a MuJoCo simulation of the stock Unitree G1, with the published motor ratings and a 10 ms motor lag. The box is 20 cm high. Nothing on this page is a hardware result.

The count is a frozen list of 100 trials. A trial passes only when the same controller lands and holds at both the 1 ms timestep and the 0.5 ms timestep. The current controller passes 49. It falls on 21. The other 30 stay up and still miss the hold: the feet hop, a joint runs through its stop, the body is not quiet at the start, or the feet drift during the hold. Every one of those 72 misses is below, plus the undisturbed landing at both timesteps so there is something to compare against.

The camera is fixed, from the side, in real time. The label on the frame is the trial, the timestep, and FALL, FAIL, or HOLDS. Scrub the bar if a fall happens quickly.
