## Methodological note

- TPM threshold follows Taylor 2021 (CeNGEN) convention.
- This catalog is reference supplementary data; peptide 
  inclusion in the simulator's T4-5 modulator expansion 
  requires additional phenotype verification (C1) + 
  receptor target coverage (A3) + Mode prediction (B4).
- CeNGEN under-detects peptides with rapid mRNA turnover 
  (e.g., NLP-22) and rate-limiting NT-synthesis enzymes 
  (tph-1, cat-2, tbh-1). Absence here does not equal 
  absence in biology.
