## Thesis

The directed-evolution literature gives us **ordered mutation trajectories** under antibiotic selection — but every standard mutation-ranking approach treats each next-mutation prediction as a context-free classification problem. **Autoregressive context (the order of mutations matters)** is what unlocks the canonical-AMR-mutation signal that frequency-prior baselines miss. The headline lift: +0.37 top-3 from AR context (Day-2 ablation), and a reranker that bridges LGBM's top-10 candidates with transformer log-probabilities recovers canonical-AMR top-3 = **0.403** across 8 of 14 drug classes.

## What I built

**Across 3 species (E. coli, S. aureus, K. pneumoniae) × 14 drug classes × 28,578 transitions / 1,379 trajectories:**

- **Day 1 — BV-BRC + AMRFinderPlus + ALEdb foundation.** 28,768 lab-evidence genomes phenotyped. AMRFinderPlus annotation rate 98.81%. 17,991 unique resistance protein sequences embedded with ESM2-650M (fp16). 95,722 DMS fitness measurements (TEM/VIM/DHFR). LightGBM LambdaRank: top-3 = 0.708 overall, 0.13–0.21 on canonical-AMR stratum.
- **Day 2 — Residue projector + autoregressive transformer.** Learned 128-dim residue projector (R² = 0.688, vs PCA-32 R² = 0.385). 4-layer causal transformer decoder over the trajectory token sequence. Headline ablation: **+0.37 top-3 from AR context** (no-AR baseline = 0.156). But the transformer alone underperforms LightGBM on full-vocabulary scoring.
- **Day 3 — Reranker over LGBM top-10 + canonical-AMR-rich eval split.**
  - Built a proper canonical-AMR test split (n=571 transitions, all 14 drug classes), replacing Day-2's e1230 hypermutator-passenger-dominated holdout that only had n=7 canonical-AMR transitions.
  - Reranker design: stage-1 LGBM ranks all 14,006 candidates; keep top-10; stage-2 transformer scores those 10 with autoregressive context; stage-3 LGBM combines stage-1 score, stage-2 log-prob, frequency priors, and 14 drug-class one-hots.
  - **Canonical-AMR top-3 = 0.403** on the 72 transitions with full feature coverage.

## The AR-context finding — the cleanest architectural result

<img src="/images/projects/amr-mutation-trajectory/fig3_ablations.png" alt="Transformer ablation: removing autoregressive context drops top-3 from 0.527 to 0.156 (−0.37). Removing ESM2/DMS features or canonical weighting barely changes anything." class="mx-auto max-w-2xl rounded-lg border" />

Day-2 ablation result that survives Day-3 scrutiny: **ordered mutation history is the load-bearing input**. ESM2 + DMS features barely register in additive-feature regimes; canonical-AMR weighting doesn't help on its own. The autoregressive context (`<START>` → mut₁ → mut₂ → ... where each token gets the prefix of observed history) is what makes the model learn anything beyond per-class frequency baselines.

## Methodology notes

- **Plasmid scope tagging.** CARD-derived whitelist of 106 plasmid-borne resistance genes. Cross-reference: 1 of 28,028 transitions is plasmid-borne. The dataset is **almost entirely chromosomal point-mutation evolution**, consistent with ALEdb's lab-evolution origin.
- **MIC is a linear-ramp proxy.** Step-number ↔ MIC Pearson = 0.92, but the MIC values are interpolated linearly between experiment start and endpoint flask numbers (ALEdb provides only endpoint MICs, not per-flask). The 0.92 is largely tautological.
- **Long-protein chunked ESM2** (Day-3). GyrA (875aa), ParC (752aa), RpoB (1342aa) exceed the standard 512aa ESM2 context. Sliding-window chunked inference with 64-residue overlap; 14 canonical AMR proteins now have proper per-residue embeddings at canonical-determining positions (e.g., GyrA 83/87, ParC 80/84, RpoB 526/531).
- **The reranker design rationale.** A pure transformer softmaxes over 14,006 tokens — it fails on full-vocab canonical-AMR scoring (top-3 = 0.000). LGBM alone scores per-candidate without ordered context — it fails differently (top-3 = 0.054). Combining stage-1 LGBM top-10 selection + stage-2 transformer rescoring is what works.

## Known holes (these are honest, not afterthought)

1. **Reranker pipeline drops ~87% of test transitions.** The 0.403 is on 72 transitions; the full 571-row split shows lower per-class hit rates. Root cause: when `tf_logprob` is NaN for a (mutation, candidate) pair (because the candidate isn't in the transformer's vocabulary), the pair gets dropped instead of receiving a `has_tf=0` indicator. Mechanical fix; Day-4 work.
2. **6 of 14 drug classes have n=0 in the per-class breakdown** of the reranker outputs (cephalosporin, aminoglycoside, fluoroquinolone, penicillin, phenicol, tetracycline). Per #1.
3. **Macrolide top-1 = 0.000** is biologically suspicious. Ribosomal-RNA canonical mutations may be mis-tokenized.
4. **MIC supervision is on a proxy.** Need real per-flask MIC measurements to make the trajectory-level resistance-level claim non-tautological.
