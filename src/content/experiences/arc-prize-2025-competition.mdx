Attempt at the [ARC-AGI](https://arcprize.org/) benchmark — the François Chollet-authored test suite that requires solving novel visual reasoning puzzles from a handful of training examples each, designed to be genuinely resistant to the standard deep-learning playbook.

Iterating on a staged pipeline approach: symmetry and transformation inference across training pairs, multi-phase candidate generation (symbolic + program-space + learned priors), scored by IoU and exact match against the public evaluation set. After eleven iterations it solved 0 of 172 evaluation tasks exactly; paused. Writeup: [ARC Prize 2025 project page](/projects/arc-prize-2025).
