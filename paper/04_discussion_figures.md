## 5. Discussion

### 5.1 A topology-dominant, gene-modulatory account of local wiring

Three method families of increasing nonlinearity converge on a single reading. **Linearly**, canonical-correlation analysis finds that transcriptional identity and local connectivity-motif participation share substantial structure: the three leading canonical correlations are ρ = [0.875, 0.836, 0.809] `[src: /home/rohit/Desktop/C-Elegans/notebooks/archive/nca/NCA.ipynb, cell 35, executed output "Canonical correlations (expr <-> motif): [0.875, 0.836, 0.809]"]`. **Statically**, a graph neural cellular automaton (GNCA) predicting per-neuron outgoing synaptic strength reaches r = 0.987 with topology + expression, but a graph-only model already reaches r ≈ 0.86, and permuting which expression vector belongs to which neuron — while leaving the graph and the expression *distribution* untouched — costs only ≈0.05 in r (0.987 → 0.934) `[src: NCA.ipynb cells 4, 5, 8; CLAUDE_CONTEXT.md lines 38–41; saved predictions /home/rohit/Desktop/C-Elegans/analysis/gnca/gnca_pred_outgoing_strength.csv]`. **Dynamically**, a gene-conditioned hybrid graph-LSTM shows no shuffle-robust improvement from real versus permuted expression (`gene_causal_by_shuffle = False`) `[src: /home/rohit/Desktop/C-Elegans/notebooks/archive/phases/PhaseD.ipynb, cell 68 verdict; cell 66 "hybrid: 0.9879, no_gene: 0.9857, gene_shuf: 0.9879"]`. And the redundancy analysis (§5.5, Fig. 1) shows the expression contribution is diffuse, surviving removal of the 200 top driver genes.

The convergent interpretation is that **local wiring in *C. elegans* is largely self-determining at the level of topology, with gene expression acting as a modulatory refinement layer rather than the primary architect.** This is *not* the claim that genes do not build the nervous system — they obviously do, through the combinatorial terminal-selector programs that fix and maintain neuron-type identity [Hobert 2016] and the surface-molecule logic that prior work has begun to read out [Barabási & Barabási 2020; Kovács et al. 2020]. It is the narrower, quantitative claim: *once the connectome's local topology is given, the additional predictive information that single-cell expression carries about that same local wiring is small, and for dynamics on this dataset it is undetectable above shuffle.*

The most natural reconciliation with the genetic-blueprint program is **temporal**: the genetic code acts largely *upstream*, sculpting topology during development, so that in the adult the topology is a near-sufficient statistic for local wiring. Under that reading, expression and connectivity are not independent (CCA confirms they are not), but expression's causal work has already been "compiled into" the graph by the time we read both modalities out — leaving only a modulatory residue measurable at the adult snapshot.

### 5.2 Why the classifier result and the wiring result are not in tension

A reader might object that an expression-only classifier recovers coarse behavioral role (sensory / interneuron / motor) at 92.7% mean cross-validated accuracy `[src: NCA.ipynb cell 28, executed output "Mean Accuracy (role): 0.9274603174603175"]` — surely that means expression "knows" connectivity? The resolution is the crux of the paper. Expression is *richly* informative about **what kind of neuron a cell is**; it is *not* correspondingly informative about **which specific partners it wires to, beyond what topology already encodes**. Knowing a neuron is a sensory cell (which expression tells you well) is a coarse-grained fact that is largely redundant with the neuron's position in the graph; it does not pin down the fine local wiring that the GNCA is scored on. The classifier is therefore an *upper-bound* probe — it establishes the strongest sense in which "expression encodes identity" — and it is precisely against that strong identity signal that the *failure* of expression to add wiring/dynamics information becomes interpretable rather than merely a weak-feature artifact.

Two honesty caveats attach to the 92.7% and must travel with it (§5.6, Limitations): the role labels are a neuron-name-prefix heuristic assigned inside the notebook (not curated WormAtlas annotations), with an `AS`-prefix collision that places `AS*` neurons in the motor class, and the cell prints no permutation/balanced-accuracy baseline `[src: NCA.ipynb cell 28]`. A sibling clean-pipeline notebook runs a *different* classification task — 3-class neurotransmitter identity (cholinergic / GABAergic / glutamatergic) at 0.727 ± 0.077 with a proper permutation null (null 95th-pct 0.629), N = 84 `[src: /home/rohit/Desktop/C-Elegans/New Notebooks/04_synthetic_dynamics_and_classification.ipynb, Part B]`. We report the 92.7% role result as the headline because it is the expression→role probe, but we do **not** conflate it with the NT task, and we flag that it lacks an in-cell null.

### 5.3 Why the honest null *strengthens* the thesis rather than weakening it

The decisive result of this study is a negative one, and we foreground it deliberately. The hypothesis entering the dynamics phase was the strong, blueprint-flavored claim that *gene expression causally shapes how neurons fire together*. The project's own pre-specified shuffle battery falsified it: the hybrid model is no better with real expression than with permuted expression (mse_hybrid = mse_gene_shuf = 0.9879 to four decimals; zeroing genes entirely is *marginally better* at 0.9857), so the causal gate (mse_shuf − mse_hybrid > 0.005) evaluates to 0.0000 → False `[src: PhaseD.ipynb cells 66, 68]`. The cleaner re-implementation reaches the *same* null by an independent preregistered criterion: gene_causal_delta = mse_shuf − mse_hybrid = 0.001283 < 0.01 → "NOT GENE-CAUSAL (null)" `[src: New Notebooks/04_synthetic_dynamics_and_classification.ipynb, Part A; summary data_derived/nb04_dynamics_summary.csv]`.

This is exactly the evidence that converts the topology-dominant claim from "an over-expressive graph model can fit anything" into a credible scientific conclusion. The danger with a high-capacity model that reaches r = 0.987 is that it could be ascribing the result to whatever feature it is fed. The shuffle control is the antidote: it asks whether the *correspondence* between a neuron and its real expression vector matters, holding the graph and the expression distribution fixed. When that correspondence is destroyed and the model barely notices (static: 0.987 → 0.934; dynamics: no change at all), the parsimonious reading is that the model is reading topology, not molecular identity. A study that reported only the r = 0.987 and the CCA coupling would be *weaker*, not stronger, than one that also reports where correctly-assigned expression fails to help. The triangulation — linear, nonlinear-static, dynamic — plus the matched nulls is the entire epistemic basis of the thesis.

We state the null at exactly the level it was earned (and no further; see §5.6): the PhaseD dynamics test was run on a **simulated** trajectory (`PhaseA_A9_trajectories_continuous.npy`), not on real calcium imaging, because the only real whole-brain dynamics set of the relevant kind (Kato 2015) lacks the neuron identities needed to align gene vectors `[src: New Notebooks/04_synthetic_dynamics_and_classification.ipynb, cell 0 notes]`. The null therefore bounds *what this clean pipeline can demonstrate on the available data*; it does not refute a gene→dynamics relationship in the animal. That distinction is part of the honesty, not a hedge against it.

### 5.4 The marginal-value framing, read against the ceiling

A caution on interpreting correlations near the ceiling. At r = 0.987 vs 0.934 the *variance-explained* gap (R² ≈ 0.974 vs 0.872) is larger than the raw-r difference looks; but it remains modest next to the graph-only baseline (r ≈ 0.86), and the ranking topology ≫ expression-correspondence is what carries the argument, not the exact marginal magnitude. The headline r = 0.987 is, moreover, an **in-sample full-fit, not a held-out estimate** — the GNCA synaptic-strength cell trains and evaluates Pearson r on the full node set with no train/test split (KFold/`train_test_split` appear only in the later motif/classifier cells), and the project's own scorecard labels the model "GNCA (full)" `[src: NCA.ipynb cells 3–8 contain no split; CLAUDE_CONTEXT.md line 38 "GNCA (full) … r = 0.987, MAE = 0.097"]`. We therefore report it as an in-sample fit (correcting the project page, which calls it "held-out"). Crucially, the comparison that carries the thesis is unaffected: the shuffle control (0.934), graph-only (≈0.86) and expression-only (≈0.27) baselines are all measured the *same* in-sample way, so the *relative* gap structure is valid even though the absolute r is not held-out. A genuinely cross-validated number does exist for a related motif-regression task — 5-fold CV average r = 0.639 (folds 0.62 / 0.25 / 0.82 / 0.86 / 0.64) `[src: NCA.ipynb cell 24]` — and a variance partition for motif prediction shows expression adds little *unique* variance over degree: degree-only R² = 0.838, expression-only R² = 0.388, both = 0.882, expression-unique = 0.044, degree-unique = 0.494 `[src: NCA.ipynb cell 31]`. The honest summary is therefore stronger, not weaker, than the single headline scalar: under proper cross-validation and variance partitioning, the topology-dominant pattern holds, and expression's *unique* marginal contribution is small (≈0.04 R²).

### 5.5 Genetic redundancy: the collapse curve

The in-silico knockout sweep (Fig. 1) characterizes *how* expression information is distributed across genes. Ranking genes by their contribution to the predictor and removing them cumulatively, model correlation drops from the trained baseline r ≈ 0.818 over the first ~10 knockouts and then **plateaus around r ≈ 0.665–0.687, holding essentially flat from K ≈ 10 through K = 200**, never approaching the chance line; the average probability the model assigns to hub neurons declines only gently (0.952 → 0.886) over the same range `[src: /home/rohit/Desktop/C-Elegans/notebooks/archive/phases/phaseE.ipynb, cell 6, executed K-sweep: K=0→0.818, K=1→0.819, K=10→0.678, K=50→0.665, K=200→0.687]`. This graceful, plateauing degradation is the signature of a **redundant** genetic code, not a few load-bearing master genes: no small driver set is individually necessary for the topology-aligned prediction. Redundancy of this kind is precisely what one expects if topology — not a compact gene set — is doing the structural work, with expression spread thinly across many partially-substitutable contributors.

Two scope notes must travel with this figure. First, the knockout *task* here is **hub-status prediction** (Hub_Div = 1 if out-degree > 5, a DirectMLP 2000→32→1 with L1 regularization on the top-2000-variance genes, N = 222 common neurons), **not** the GNCA synaptic-strength regression `[src: phaseE.ipynb cell 6]`. The collapse curve therefore evidences redundancy in the **expression→hub-topology map**, and we describe it as such, not as redundancy in the GNCA edge model. Second, its baseline r ≈ 0.82 is the operating point of *that* driver-attribution model and must not be conflated with the GNCA's r = 0.987 — they are different setups; the curve is informative about the *shape* of degradation (redundancy structure), not about absolute predictive ceiling.

### 5.6 Relationship to prior work

Our finding is compatible with — and sharpens — the existing literature. Arnatkevičiūtė et al. [2018] showed that connected hub neurons are genomically distinct beyond lineage, birth time, neurotransmitter system, and spatial position; Fenyves et al. [2020] showed that pre-/post-synaptic neurotransmitter and receptor expression predicts synaptic *sign* for two-thirds of ionotropic chemical synapses. We do not contradict either: CCA (ρ up to 0.875) reproduces the coupling. What we add is the **conditional accounting** — the marginal value of expression *given topology* — and the matched shuffle/ablation controls that isolate it. The Barabási genetic-connectome program [Barabási & Barabási 2020; Kovács et al. 2020] argues that genetic identity predicts connectome motifs and can infer gap-junction (innexin) rules; our results suggest that for *local synaptic-strength* prediction the topology already encodes most of that motif structure, so a topology-aware predictor recovers the signal without explicit gene labels. The two views reconcile if the genetic program is largely upstream (§5.1): genes set the topology during development, and at the adult stage topology is a near-sufficient statistic for local wiring.

## 6. Limitations

We separate **scope/design limits** (intrinsic to what the study can claim) from **provenance/rigor gaps** (fixable before submission, flagged honestly).

### 6.1 Scope and design limits

1. **Local and static by construction.** The GNCA is intentionally a *local* propagator over a neighborhood; this paper speaks to *local* wiring, not to global developmental specification or long-range routing. A genetic blueprint may dominate at scales this design cannot see.
2. **Adult snapshot.** Both modalities are essentially adult. Developmental connectome series [Witvliet et al. 2021] could reveal a stronger gene-causal phase that is no longer visible once wiring is complete — consistent with the "genes act upstream" reconciliation, which this study cannot itself confirm.
3. **Correlational, not causal.** Every result here is observational. The shuffle controls establish that expression *correspondence* adds little predictive value over topology; they do not establish a causal direction. The in-silico "knockout" is an ablation of model inputs, not a biological perturbation.
4. **Dataset-specific null on simulated dynamics (§5.3).** The `gene_causal_by_shuffle = False` result was obtained on a *simulated* trajectory, because the real dynamics set lacks neuron identities. "Absence of detectable improvement" is not "proof of no effect": the dynamics data are sparse, the model family finite, and a more powerful model or richer (identity-resolved) recordings could surface a gene→dynamics signal. The null bounds what this pipeline can show, not biology.
5. **Hermaphrodite somatic neurons only.** The analysis is restricted to the hermaphrodite; the male-specific nervous system and the pharyngeal nervous system are out of scope. Generalization across sex and across the full 302/391-neuron complement is untested.
6. **Expression representation dependence.** Results may depend on the embedding (top-32 PCA of StandardScaler-normalized CeNGEN expression for the GNCA; PCA(50) for CCA), on quantitative vs. binary presence/absence encoding, and on gene-set choice. Robustness across representations is future work.

## 8. Note on the methods-paper split

The GNCA of §3.2 is, independently of the worm biology, a method for **connectome-constrained synaptic-property prediction**: a message-passing model run in the cellular-automaton regime [Mordvintsev et al. 2020] over a directed multigraph with optional node features, evaluated under a structured **topology-vs-feature ablation protocol**. Concretely the architecture is a 64-dim per-node hidden state initialized via tanh(Linear(32→64)) over top-32 PCA expression features, with 8 message-passing/update steps (message = out-degree-normalized adjacency × Linear(64→64); residual MLP update Linear(128→128)→ReLU→Linear(128→64) over concat[state, message]; stochastic Bernoulli(0.5) per-node "fire" mask during training; LayerNorm+Linear(64→1) readout), ≈0.031M parameters, trained AdamW (lr 3e-3, wd 1e-4) + cosine schedule, MSE on z-scored mean outgoing synaptic strength, seed 42 `[src: NCA.ipynb cells 1–3]`.

That methodological contribution — the architecture, the **shuffle-control evaluation design** (full / shuffled-feature / graph-only / feature-only), and the held-out-edge (after the cohort/CV fixes of §6.2) regression setup — is naturally separable from the *C. elegans* biology and is a clean fit for a graph-representation-learning venue (**NeurIPS GRL workshop** or **ICLR LMRL** — Learning Meaningful Representations of Life). We anticipate a short companion methods paper presenting the GNCA and its feature-vs-structure ablation protocol as a reusable tool for biological-graph property prediction, benchmarked against standard GNN baselines [Gilmer et al. 2017; Kipf & Welling 2017]. The two papers share the §5.1/Fig. 2 ablation but differ in framing: **here** it is evidence about worm biology (does molecular identity add information over topology?); **there** it is a benchmark of an architecture against feature-vs-structure baselines. Two preconditions gate the methods split, both already identified: (i) the cohort definition must be cleaned and fixed (§6.2 item 7) so the benchmark runs on a well-specified node set, and (ii) the evaluation must be moved to genuinely held-out edges with CIs (§6.2 items 9–10), since a methods venue will scrutinize the train/test protocol more sharply than an application venue.
