## Thesis

A coverage-robust, atlas-aware cfDNA cancer-detection classifier can be built from **public-data-only sources** (no dbGaP / no EGA) by training a learned NNLS-style deconvolution over the Loyfer 2023 atlas, augmenting with TCGA tumor methylation profiles via per-cancer Gaussian-mixture sampling, and exposing the classifier to coverage-degraded variants during training. The Day-2 to Day-3 cross-cohort AUROC jump on GSE122126 (0.217 → **0.850**) is what this stack buys.

## What I built

**Pipeline over 23 deep-WGBS healthy cfDNA samples (GSE186458, ~140× coverage) + 108 TCGA tumor tissue samples across COAD/LIHC/LUAD/BRCA, deconvolved against the Loyfer 2023 atlas (9520 atlas blocks × 40 cell types):**

- **Day 1 — NNLS deconvolution validation.** Granulocyte fraction = 29.2% (Loyfer-published: ~30%). 828 in-silico mixtures across 5 cancer types × 9 tumor fractions. Day-1 multitask MLP: stage-3 sens 36.5% @ 99% spec, tissue top-1 58.3%. Cross-cohort AUROC on GSE122126: **0.217** (model inverting rankings).
- **Day 2 — Learned deep deconvolution + fragmentomics fusion.** Beats NNLS on in-silico mixtures (Pearson 0.999 vs 0.972). Megakaryocyte ≈ 28% finding confirmed as real platelet-derived signal (Fox-Fisher 2022). Cross-cohort still failing.
- **Day 3 — the four mechanisms that closed the cross-cohort gap.** 0.217 → **0.850**:
  1. **Distribution-sampled mixtures.** Per-cancer Gaussian-mixture models over TCGA tumor methylation, with Loyfer-atlas centroid fallback at the 91.6% of atlas blocks TCGA's 450K array doesn't cover.
  2. **NaN-aware learned deconvolution.** Input expanded from 9520-dim betas to 19,040-dim (betas-imputed ‖ observation mask). Trained with random 10–80% block masking.
  3. **Coverage-augmentation training.** Generate deconv features at 4 coverage levels {100, 30, 15, 8}%, training over identical labels → classifier learns coverage-invariant boundaries.
  4. **TCGA-tissue + Loyfer-atlas fallback.** At TCGA-observed blocks (~8.4% atlas coverage), sample from the per-block Gaussian. At unobserved blocks, use the Loyfer atlas's tumor-tissue centroid as the prior.

## Coverage robustness — what coverage augmentation actually buys

<img src="/images/projects/cfdna-cancer-detection/fig3_coverage_robustness.png" alt="Coverage-stratified in-silico AUROC: 0.95 (100%) → 0.88 (30%) → 0.83 (15%) → 0.80 (8%). Sensitivity @ 99% specificity tracks the same shape." class="mx-auto max-w-2xl rounded-lg border" />

The 8–15% atlas coverage regime is where GSE122126 (clinical 450K array) lives. Without coverage augmentation, the classifier saturates and inverts; with it, AUROC degrades gracefully and the operating threshold remains usable.

## Methodology notes

- **The Day-3 v1 → v2 fix.** First Day-3 attempt collapsed tumor profiles to healthy means at TCGA-unobserved blocks (91.6% of atlas). Fix: sample per-block Gaussian where TCGA observed, fall back to Loyfer atlas centroid where TCGA blind. v2 preserves the tumor signal in the atlas-aware regions.
- **The Day-3 collapse fix.** First NaN-aware training collapsed to "Megakaryocyte" for all samples because healthy NNLS gives Megak ≈ Granul ≈ 0.27 and a poorly-conditioned CE-on-dominant-cell-type pulled the argmax to whichever class won the first batch. Fix: drop CE-on-dominant, use MSE + KL over full 40-dim fraction vector.
- **The score-saturation issue.** Cross-cohort scores fall in [0.894, 0.9998] — sigmoid saturated. AUROC is real (rank-order correct), but the 99%-spec operating threshold collapses. Calibration (Platt or temperature scaling) is the Day-4 priority.

## What this work isn't

Honest read after a literature check: **the four mechanisms above are not novel** at the level of individual components. M-PACT (Nature Cancer 2026) does coverage-sparsity augmentation training over 3.2M in-silico samples for pediatric CNS tumors. CUPiD (Nature Communications 2024) does TCGA-tumor + cfDNA in-silico mixtures across 29 tumor classes (n=143 + n=27 validation). crossNN (Nature Cancer 2025) is explicitly cross-platform DNA-methylation-based tumor classification with the same masking strategy. MetDecode (Bioinformatics 2024) is coverage-aware deconvolution. The generalizable HCC framework (bioRxiv 2025) hits the same score-saturation issue and uses threshold tuning, the same Day-4 priority I'd write.

What this project *does* contribute:

1. **Public-data-only reproducibility.** No dbGaP, no EGA, no commercial cohort. Everything is GEO + TCGA + Loyfer, with all preprocessing scripts in `src/`.
2. **Explicit failure-mode documentation.** LUAD 0.56, tissue-top-1 regression 0.811 → 0.247 from multi-task pooling, score saturation breaking the 99%-spec threshold — these are all in the report rather than swept under the rug.
3. **A clean four-component ablation** of the cross-cohort fix.
